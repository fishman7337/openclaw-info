PERSONAL ASSISTANT

Kun Ming OpenClaw

A private assistant running on its owner’s computer. It helps organize inbox email, answer routine messages, and check calendar availability before arranging meetings.

This is a personal-use application. It is not offered as a service to the public.

The assistant connects the owner’s Google and Telegram accounts and uses the owner’s OpenAI account to understand requests. Archived messages and chats are excluded from its workflows.
