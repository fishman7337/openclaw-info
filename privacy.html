Kun Ming OpenClaw

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Kun Ming OpenClaw is a private, personal-use assistant operated by Goh Kun Ming on the owner’s computer. It does not provide public account registration.

Access and purpose

With the owner’s authorization, the assistant reads individual inbox emails and their labels, adds email categories, and sends authorized messages. It reads calendar availability and creates calendar events for authorized scheduling. It connects to the owner’s Telegram bot and personal account for instructions and routine messaging. When linked by the owner, WhatsApp is used for routine personal messaging as well. Its workflows exclude archived emails and chats and check calendar availability before booking.

Processing and sharing

Relevant message and calendar information may be sent to OpenAI through the owner’s account to interpret requests and prepare actions. Google handles Gmail and Calendar operations; Telegram handles Telegram messages and account access. WhatsApp handles WhatsApp device linking and messages when that connection is enabled. These providers process information under the terms and account settings applicable to their services. Information is sent to other recipients only as part of the owner’s authorized correspondence or scheduling. The assistant does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop a generalized AI model.

Storage and retention

Authentication material, local sessions, task history, and processing records are stored on the owner’s computer. Access to connection files is restricted to the local user. Local records remain until removed by the owner. Provider-side retention follows the provider’s applicable terms and account settings.

Control and revocation

The owner may pause background processing, stop the assistant, revoke Google access through Google Account settings, terminate the Telegram session through Telegram settings, and remove local assistant data. Revoking access prevents further account operations; it does not itself remove previously stored local records or messages already sent.
